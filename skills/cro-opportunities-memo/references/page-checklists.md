# Page checklists: trust and CTA

Folded in from the retired `ai-powered-cro-analysis` skill (Oct 2026). Use these as the checklist when a finding touches trust signals or CTA hierarchy, in any of the three deliverables. They are prompts for what to look at, not findings. Every item still has to pass the evidence tiers in `core.md` before it ships.

## Trust signals

- Reviews: which platform (Trustpilot, Google, native)? Is the star rating visible sitewide?
- Press and media mentions: present? Above or below the fold?
- Certifications and badges: SSL, payment security, industry certs.
- Returns policy: easy to find? Placed near the buy decision?
- About and story: is there a human brand behind the product?
- UGC (customer photos): on the PDP or homepage?
- Guarantees: money-back, satisfaction, quality promise.
- Social proof from social media: follower counts, embedded feed, mentions.

## CTA hierarchy and visual flow

- Primary CTA: is there one clear primary action per page?
- Color contrast: does the button stand out from the background?
- CTA copy: action-led and specific? ("Add to Bag" beats "Continue.")
- Secondary CTAs: do they compete with the primary or support it?
- Visual hierarchy: does the eye travel toward the CTA?
- Whitespace: does the CTA have room to stand out?
- Repetition: is the CTA repeated at key scroll depths?
- Above the fold: is the primary CTA visible without scrolling? (Fold claims are viewport-conditional, see `core.md`.)
- Button size: big enough to be the dominant element?
- Urgency in the CTA: does the copy give a reason to act now? Credible urgency only.

## What this audit can't tell you

Close every markdown teardown with this footer. It keeps the reader honest about what a page review can and can't prove.

```
What this audit can't tell you:
- Which fix will move revenue most. That needs an A/B test.
- Whether this is your main bottleneck. That needs traffic and funnel data.
- How your audience responds emotionally. That needs session recordings and user research.
```
